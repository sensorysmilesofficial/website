Meet the Team

Founder & Project Lead

Meet Shi Qi

Hi everyone! My name is Shi Qi Ooi, founder of Sensory Smiles. I am a Y11 student in CISHK and I firmly believe in the importance of relieving sensory distress and providing calm and comfort for all. Since I was a child, I always had a love for toys and felt the need to fidget with something, so Sensory Smiles is my way of bridging these two characteristics and ensuring access for all. Beyond excited to see where this goes and excited to create more lasting change!
